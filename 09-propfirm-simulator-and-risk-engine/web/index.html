The Mathematical Reality of Prop Firm Evaluations

Prop firm rules are engineered around asymmetric absorbing barriers. A retail trader risking 1.5% to 2.0% per trade faces a 93% to 97% mathematical failure rate not because their strategy lacks edge, but because the probability of 3 consecutive intraday losses is over 12%, immediately triggering daily drawdown termination.

Rule 1: The 4x Daily Safety Sizing Rule

Never risk more than 1/4th of your daily loss limit on any single trade. If daily DD is 4%, maximum risk is 0.50% to 0.75%.

Rule 2: The Two-Loss Daily Circuit Breaker

If you suffer 2 consecutive losses in a single day (-1.0%), shut down the terminal. Continuing to trade creates revenge risk and accounts for 82% of all daily limit breaches.

Rule 3: Phase 2 De-risking Protocol

Phase 2 target is typically only 5%. Once you are within 1.5% of passing, cut your risk per trade by 50% (e.g. from 0.5% down to 0.25%) to eliminate endgame variance.

Rule 4: Neutralize Trailing Peak Drawdown

On intraday trailing firms (Apex, Bulenox), take partial profits early. Never let open floating profits retrace, as the trailing drawdown ratchets up with peak unrealized equity.
