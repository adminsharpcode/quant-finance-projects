Why does it seem to "print money"? It is powered by three mathematical pillars:

Because you are a net seller of options, time is your ally. Every second that ticks by, options lose extrinsic time value according to the Black-76 Theta equation:

ΘBlack-76 = - [ e-rT × F × N'(d1) × σ ] / [ 2 √T ] - r × e-rT × K × N(±d2)

Even if the price of Gold does not move an inch, the positions lose value, which means your short sale gains profit automatically every single day!

3. Directional Immunity via Delta Neutrality (Δ = 0)

An ATM Call has $\Delta \approx +0.50$. An ATM Put has $\Delta \approx -0.50$. When you sell both:

Δstraddle = (-1 × +0.50) + (-1 × -0.50) = -0.50 + 0.50 = 0.000 !

Small price movements in Gold have zero net directional effect on your portfolio!

The Catch: Negative Gamma Risk (Γ < 0)
Is it 100% risk-free? NO! The cost of positive Theta is negative Gamma. Taylor series expansion reveals:
ΔPnL ≈ Δ × ΔF + 1/2 Γ × (ΔF)2 + Θ × Δt
When $\Delta = 0$, you earn $\Theta \times \Delta t > 0$. But if Gold suffers a massive surprise geopolitical jump ($\Delta F = \pm $100$), the quadratic term $\frac{1}{2}\Gamma(\Delta F)^2$ turns deeply negative! That is why professional desks run automated Delta Rebalancing (Neutralize Δ) to re-hedge when delta drifts.
