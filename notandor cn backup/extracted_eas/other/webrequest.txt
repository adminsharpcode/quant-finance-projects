Adding required servers in the list of allowed URLs

Open your MT4/MT5 terminal, go to the "Tools" menu and select “Options”. You can also press Ctrl+O to get to this menu quickly next time. Click on the “Expert Advisors“ tab. Here you should allow requests to:


https://ec.forexprostools.com/
https://nfs.faireconomy.media/ff_calendar_thisweek.json
https://cdn-nfs.faireconomy.media/ff_calendar_thisweek.json
http://nfs.faireconomy.media/ff_calendar_thisweek.xml 
https://www.worldtimeserver.com
https://nfs.faireconomy.media
